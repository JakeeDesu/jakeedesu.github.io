Projects Overview Staging is a personal application for its approved owner. Staging is used with synthetic data to verify application behavior and recovery.

Access and use

Use of the private workspace is restricted to its approved owner. Google consent or sign-in by another account does not grant workspace access. These public information pages provide no access to private records or backups.

Backup and recovery

The owner is responsible for keeping a separate, protected recovery-key copy and maintaining the account authorizations needed for backups. Encrypted archives require the recovery key to decrypt. A successful recovery check verifies the tested backup; it does not guarantee every future run will succeed.

Revoking Google Drive authorization stops future authorized Drive access. It does not itself remove existing encrypted files. The owner manages retained files and data separately.

Data practices

The Privacy Policy describes the application's Google permissions, data processing and retention. These pages offer no paid service or public account registration.
