Personal project & task tracker

A private place to keep projects moving.

Projects Overview Staging helps its approved owner organize projects, tasks, notes and attachments. Google sign-in identifies the owner, and access is restricted to that owner.

01

Project organization

Keep project plans, tasks, notes and related files together in a private workspace.

02

Owner access

Google sign-in supports authentication. Signing in with another Google account does not grant access to the workspace.

03

Encrypted backups

A separate Google Drive permission manages backups created by the application. Archives are encrypted before upload, and the owner keeps a recovery key separately.

About this staging application

Staging uses synthetic data to verify application behavior and backup recovery. This information site describes the application and its data practices. Workspace records and backup files are private.

Read the Privacy Policy for Google permissions, storage and retention, and the Terms of Use for the intended use of the application.
